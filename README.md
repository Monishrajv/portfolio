## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Download/Clone** the portfolio files to your local machine
2. **Open** `index.html` in your web browser
3. **Customize** the content to match your information
4. **Deploy** to your preferred hosting platform

## 🎨 Customization Guide

### Personal Information
Edit the following sections in `index.html`:

- **Hero Section**: Update name, title, and description
- **Contact Information**: Update email, phone, location, and LinkedIn
- **About Section**: Modify the about text and statistics
- **Education**: Update your educational background
- **Skills**: Add or remove skills and technologies
- **Experience**: Update internship and work experience
- **Projects**: Add your projects with descriptions and technologies
- **Certificates**: List your certifications
- **Leadership**: Add your extracurricular activities
- **Interests**: Update your hobbies and interests

### Styling
Modify `styles.css` to customize:

- **Colors**: Change the color scheme by updating CSS variables
- **Fonts**: Replace Google Fonts with your preferred fonts
- **Layout**: Adjust spacing, padding, and grid layouts
- **Animations**: Modify transition effects and timing

### Functionality
Update `script.js` to customize:

- **Form Handling**: Modify contact form submission logic
- **Animations**: Adjust scroll animations and effects
- **Navigation**: Customize mobile menu behavior
